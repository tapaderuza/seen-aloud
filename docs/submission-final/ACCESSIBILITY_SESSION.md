# Five-minute check with a blind or low-vision person, or an accessibility professional

Not done yet. Nothing below is a result. Record only what happens.

**Setup:** their own computer and screen reader (NVDA, JAWS or VoiceOver), desktop Chrome, https://seen-aloud.web.app. No Gemini Nano is needed: keep Hybrid and accept the cloud when asked.

| Minute | Task, said aloud | What to note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00 | Find and press “Try built-in sample video” | Found by heading, button list or Tab? Time taken |
| 1:00 | Press “Describe this video” and wait | Are progress and “Done” announced? Is the consent dialog clear? |
| 2:00 | Play the video | Are the descriptions heard in the pauses, at a usable speed? Does the pause feel right? |
| 3:00 | Edit the first line, then “Fit to pause” | Can the line be reached and edited? Is the word count announced? |
| 4:00 | Export WebVTT | Is the download noticed? |
| 4:30 | One question: would you use this to describe your own or your organisation’s videos, and what would stop you? | Their words, verbatim |

Ask permission before quoting anyone, and publish nothing without it.

## Request (Spanish)

Hola. Estoy terminando Seen Aloud, una herramienta web gratuita que redacta audiodescripciones editables para vídeos y describe los fotogramas en el propio ordenador. ¿Podrías dedicarme 5 minutos con tu lector de pantalla para probarla y decirme qué falla? No hace falta instalar nada: https://seen-aloud.web.app. No publicaré nada tuyo sin tu permiso. ¡Gracias!

## Request (English)

Hi. I am finishing Seen Aloud, a free web tool that drafts editable audio description for videos and describes the frames on your own computer. Could you spare 5 minutes with your screen reader to try it and tell me what breaks? Nothing to install: https://seen-aloud.web.app. I will not publish anything from you without your permission.
